# Chapter 8 — Protein, Calories, and Macros That Matter

Right — numbers. Not because you’re a robot. Because guessing forever is how you stay soft, stuck, or accidentally underfed while “eating clean.”

We’ll keep this usable on a construction schedule, a dad schedule, or any schedule where you don’t have a chef.

## 1. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

### Protein

A practical year-one target many lifters do well with:

> **Roughly 1.6–2.2 g of protein per kg of body weight per day**  
> (or about **0.7–1 g per lb**), adjusted if body weight is very high (use a goal/adjusted weight with coaching sense).

That’s a **practical band**, not a law that more is always better. On average, fat-free-mass returns from extra protein flatten around **~1.6 g/kg** for many resistance-trained adults; the upper end of the band still helps as a buffer — especially in a deficit, when leaner athletes sometimes aim higher **relative to fat-free mass** rather than treating 2.2 g/kg body weight as magic. *(Claim `clm_ed1a9a266269`; bib `bib_9914827c24`, `bib_e498488c33`, `bib_381074aa0f`.)*

In a deficit, leaning to the higher end helps muscle retention and hunger. On a bulk, you still need enough — surplus carbs won’t replace protein.

Distribute across 3–5 feedings if you can. Perfect timing is optional; total daily intake is not.

### Calories

**Step 1 — Estimate maintenance.**  
Use a calculator as a starting guess, or track weight + intake for 2 weeks and reverse-engineer. It’s an estimate. Reality is the referee.

**Step 2 — Set the phase:**

| Goal | Starting adjustment |
|------|---------------------|
| Fat loss (C, many B) | ~300–500 kcal/day below maintenance (adjust by trend) |
| Recomp / hold (many B, D) | About maintenance ± small nudge |
| Growth (many A) | ~200–400 kcal/day above maintenance; patience > dirty bulk chaos |

**Step 3 — Review every 2–4 weeks.**  
Losing too fast / strength crashing → eat more. Weight up too fast with soft photos → trim. Scale stuck but photos better → maybe stay (recomp).

### Carbs and fats

Once protein and calories are set:

- **Fats:** don’t crush to zero; include enough for adherence and hormonal health (often ~0.6–1 g/kg as a loose band — individual).  
- **Carbs:** fill the rest; bias more carbs around hard training if it helps performance.  

Macro perfectionism that makes you miserable is a bad macro plan.

### Alcohol and “flexible” foods

They count. Estimate them. Budget them. Don’t build your identity around either extreme purity or nightly obliteration.

### Home and busy-day tactics

- Rotisserie chicken, Greek yoghurt, eggs, canned tuna, whey if useful  
- Rice, potatoes, oats, fruit  
- Frozen veg  
- Same breakfast 5 days/week is a feature  

Protein powder is a **convenience** tool for hitting totals — not superior magic versus food when daily protein is matched. *(Claim `clm_808a8b7a439d`.)* Creatine belongs in supplements (Part Four) — food first here.

## 2. WHY IT MATTERS

Protein without calorie awareness can still miss fat-loss goals. Calories without protein can make you smaller in all the wrong ways. Macros without adherence are cosplay.

Getting these three roughly right is the highest-leverage nutrition work you’ll do all year.

## 3. WHAT EXPERIENCED BODYBUILDERS DO

- **Hit protein first every day.**  
- **Move calories based on trend data**, not vibes alone.  
- **Keep a few high-protein anchor meals.**  
- **Raise protein when cutting** if hunger and retention need help — many aim near that ~g/lb ballpark when dieting hard.  
- **Don’t abort a bulk at week eight** because the mirror got slightly softer — soft ≠ failure if strength and months remaining say stay.  
- **Accept estimates when eating out**; resume precision at home.

## 4. YOUR ACTION PLAN

1. **Calculate a protein target** from current or adjusted body weight; write the gram number on your logbook.  
2. **Pick calorie phase** and a starting daily calorie number (estimate OK).  
3. **Build a sample day** that hits protein + calories with foods you like.  
4. **Shop once** for the anchors.  
5. **Run 14 days**, log weight trend + training.  
6. **Adjust ±100–200 kcal** if the trend disagrees with the goal — don’t overhaul everything.

### Pathway tilt

- **A:** Prioritise hitting surplus; liquid calories if appetite is poor.  
- **B:** Protein high; calories at maintenance or mild deficit.  
- **C:** Protein high; deficit sustainable; high-volume low-calorie veg helps.  
- **D:** Fix protein consistency before exotic macro splits.

## 6. COMMON MISTAKES

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Protein “when I remember” | Anchor meals + optional shake |
| Huge surplus “dreamer bulk” | Small surplus, long block |
| Tiny crash deficit | Moderate cut, keep lifts |
| Rewriting macros daily | Stable plan, review biweekly |
| Ignoring oils/sauces | Track the sneaky calories once to learn |
