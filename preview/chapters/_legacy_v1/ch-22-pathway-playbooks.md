# Chapter 22 — Pathway Playbooks (A–D)

This is the laminated card chapter. Same science for everyone; **different first moves** depending on where you started. Use it at phase reviews and when Chapter 21 says you need a pathway-flavoured nudge.

Foundation biases below assume training/nutrition competence first. Advanced tools (Part Four) are a separate lane: smart, coached use — not a playbook substitute for protein and progressive overload.

---

## 1. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

### Playbook A — Skinny / hardgainer look

**Primary job:** get bigger while learning to lift.

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Slight surplus most days | Endless cardio that cancels intake |
| Progressive compounds 3–4×/week | Programme-hop for “shredding” tips |
| Protein hit + easy calories (shakes, denser foods) | Clean-eating maintenance forever |
| Patient photo reviews | Panic at slight softness week 8 |

**If stuck light:** raise calories; check steps/cardio; confirm you’re not in accidental deficit; keep overload honest.  
**Phase lean:** Foundation = eat + learn · Build = patient surplus · Refine = weak-point size · Peak = usually keep growing unless you truly want a short polish.

### Playbook B — Skinny-fat

**Primary job:** improve composition — tighter middle, thicker limbs.

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| High protein always | Dirty bulk “for aesthetics later” |
| Hard lifting + recomp or mild deficit | Ab-only fat-loss plans |
| Steps consistency | Live on cardio, skip legs |
| Judge photos/waist over scale drama | Weekly phase flips |

**If soft and lifts OK:** small calorie cut or steps up.  
**If flat and weak:** raise food toward maintenance; keep training hard.  
**Phase lean:** often recomp early → decide cut vs grow at month 6–9 based on photos.

### Playbook C — Overweight / higher body fat

**Primary job:** lose fat without sacrificing the muscle you’re building.

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Sustainable deficit + high protein | Crash diets that kill training |
| Lift like muscle matters | Cardio-only restarts |
| Steps before extreme hunger | All-or-nothing Mondays |
| Diet breaks when adherence dies | Shame spirals after social meals |

**If loss stalls with adherence:** steps → small further cut → check weekends.  
**If strength collapsing:** ease deficit; deload; sleep.  
**Phase lean:** adherence-first cut through Foundation/Build as needed → Refine with diet breaks → Peak decide finish vs maintain.

### Playbook D — Restart / already active

**Primary job:** fix the gaps that made prior years noisy.

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Audit protein, overload, sleep, logging | Add advanced techniques first |
| Simplify to a boring progressive template | Keep three half-followed programmes |
| Pick a nutrition bias and finish a block | Eternal “recomp” with no tracking |
| Use muscle memory — show up consistently | Compare to your old best week forever |

**If stuck:** usually honesty problems (logged effort, logged food), not secret exercises.  
**Phase lean:** audit Foundation → Build with progressive honesty → Refine on purpose → Peak with a real Year 2 periodisation plan.

## 2. WHY IT MATTERS

A good general chapter still gets misapplied. Playbooks keep your corrections **on-brand for your body**, so Pathway A stops stealing Pathway C advice and calling it discipline.

## 3. WHAT EXPERIENCED BODYBUILDERS DO

- Match phase length to goal.  
- Borrow principles across pathways without copying someone else’s calorie bias.  
- Re-choose pathway bias when photos say so — not when motivation is bored.

## 4. YOUR ACTION PLAN

1. Highlight your playbook section.  
2. Copy the Do/Don’t table into your notes.  
3. At each phase review, answer: “Am I still on the right bias?”  
4. When Chapter 21 fires, apply the matching “If stuck” line before inventing a new sport.  
5. If two pathways fit, pick the **nutrition bias** from the higher-fat or lower-muscle reality — photos break ties.
