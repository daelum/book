# Chapter 20 — Months 10–12: Peak and Decide

This isn’t a contest week unless you want it to be. It’s a **proof and fork**: prove what twelve months of systems did, then decide what Year 2 is for — without burning the playbook in a panic cut or a dreamer bulk.

## 1. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

### Phase goal

1. Maintain (or slightly polish) the physique and strength you’ve built.  
2. Run a full review vs Month 1.  
3. Choose Year 2 direction.  
4. Optionally read Part Four as **literacy**, not a starter kit.

### Peak ≠ dehydration theatre

For lifestyle bodybuilding, “peak” means:

- Consistent photos in familiar lighting  
- Best representative strength numbers (not one ugly max)  
- Waist / clothing fit notes  
- Clear summary of what actually worked  

You don’t need stage tricks. You need truth.

### Review ritual (book a real hour)

Compare Month 1 → Month 12:

| Domain | Evidence |
|--------|----------|
| Shape | Photos front/side/back |
| Midsection | Waist trend |
| Strength | Key lift log excerpts |
| Habits | Attendance %, protein consistency |
| Life fit | Did the system survive work/kids? |
| Misses | What repeatedly broke? |

Write a one-page “Year 1 post-mortem” — wins, mistakes, keepers.

### Year 2 forks (examples)

- Continue growth (especially Pathway A / still lean enough)  
- Dedicated fat-loss block with muscle retention  
- Maintenance / recomp while life is chaos  
- Performance focus (strength) with physique as constraint  
- Same pathway, better execution (often the real win)

Advanced pharmacology curiosity → Part Four education + clinician conversations — **not** a Year 1 redo button.

## 2. WHY IT MATTERS

Without a deliberate peak/decide phase, people ghost the plan at month ten, take blurry mirror selfies, and declare the year a wash. Or they crash-cut for a holiday and undo hard-won training quality. The review is how twelve months becomes twenty-four.

## 3. WHAT EXPERIENCED BODYBUILDERS DO

- Schedule the review like an appointment.  
- Celebrate boring metrics (sessions trained, protein days).  
- Avoid last-minute extreme cuts that trash lifts.  
- Carry forward the template that worked; change the bias, not the entire identity.  
- Separate “I want more” from “the method failed.”

## 4. YOUR ACTION PLAN

**Months 10–11**

1. Keep training quality high; optional slight volume trim if life stress peaks.  
2. Hold food at a bias that doesn’t create chaos before review photos.  
3. Fix sleep if it’s been the silent leak.  

**Month 12**

4. Repeat baseline protocol (Chapter 6 conditions).  
5. Fill the review table.  
6. Write Year 2 aim in one sentence + first 12-week bias.  
7. Update MED week and calendar defaults for the next season of life.  
8. Optional: skim Part Four for literacy only.

### Pathway tilt at year end

- **A:** Often “continue growth” if still light; don’t rush a cut for clout.  
- **B:** Decide whether to keep recomp, cut to reveal, or grow deliberately.  
- **C:** Decide finish-cut vs maintain vs reverse diet into training quality.  
- **D:** Periodize on purpose — you now have data; use it.

## 6. COMMON MISTAKES

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Crash cut for photos | Slight polish or maintain |
| Skipping the written review | Book the hour |
| Throwing out a working programme | Keep skeleton; change bias |
| Comparing to enhanced timelines | Compare to your Month 1 |

## 7. YOUR CHECKLIST

- [ ] Review ritual complete  
- [ ] Year 2 fork chosen

## 8. WHAT’S NEXT

Peaks still stall sometimes mid-year. **Plateaus and course corrections** — when to change food, training, or recovery — is the decision layer you’ll use every phase, not only at month twelve.
