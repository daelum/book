# Chapter 17 — Months 1–3: Foundation

Right — the year starts here. Months 1–3 are not for becoming famous in the mirror. They’re for **installing the craft**: form you can trust, food you can hit, sleep you defend, and progressive overload you can prove in a logbook.

If Phase 1 feels “too basic,” good. Basic done for twelve weeks beats advanced done for twelve days.

## 1. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

### Phase goal

Learn the movements. Hit protein and your calorie bias most days. Sleep enough to train again. Progress key lifts somehow. Take honest photos at the ends of months 1 and 3.

### Weekly skeleton (gym)

**Option A — Full body 3× (Mon/Wed/Fri or Tue/Thu/Sat)**

Each session covers: squat or leg-press pattern · hinge · horizontal press · horizontal pull · vertical push *or* pull (alternate) · 1–2 accessories (arms/delts/core).

**Option B — Upper/lower 4×**

- Lower: squat, hinge, lunge/leg press, calves, core  
- Upper: press, row, vertical pull, secondary press, arms/delts  

Sessions 45–75 minutes. Work sets near your Chapter 12 effort standard once form stabilises.

### Home / limited equipment

Same patterns with whatever progressive tools you have (dumbbells, bands + slow eccentrics, backpack loading). If load is limited, progress reps/tempo/ROM. Any tensile stimulus beats disappearing for three weeks — then return to better loading when you can.

### Nutrition bias this phase

| Pathway | Months 1–3 food bias |
|---------|----------------------|
| **A** | Eat enough — slight surplus; liquid calories if appetite is rubbish |
| **B** | High protein; maintenance or mild deficit |
| **C** | Adherence-first deficit; protein non-negotiable; steps before crash cuts |
| **D** | Fix protein + logging first; simplify food noise |

### Non-negotiables for everyone

1. Written programme (Chapter 11)  
2. Logbook every session  
3. Protein target most days  
4. Baseline + month-3 photos (same conditions)  
5. Sleep minimum from Chapter 13  
6. MED week protocol from Chapter 15 when life explodes  

### What you do *not* do in Phase 1

- Programme-hop at week three  
- Advanced intensity techniques as identity  
- Panic-cut a surplus because you feel slightly softer  
- Unsupervised drug protocols (year-one action plans stay drug-free)

## 2. WHY IT MATTERS

Foundation weeks teach the skill of being a trainee. Miss that and later “Build” and “Refine” phases just amplify chaos. Also: early strength jumps are often neural — enjoy them, log them, don’t assume the rate lasts forever.

## 3. WHAT EXPERIENCED BODYBUILDERS DO

- Keep the template boring on purpose.  
- Film lifts early while habits form.  
- Judge Phase 1 by attendance, form, and progressive overload — not stage conditioning.  
- Use the end of month 3 as a real review, not a vibe check.

## 4. YOUR ACTION PLAN

**Week 0 (setup):** pathway locked (Ch 16) · programme printed · food targets · photo/waist/weight method · gym access sorted.

**Weeks 1–4:** learn lifts; leave a bit more RIR if needed; hit protein; establish step floor.

**Weeks 5–8:** push work-set effort; double-progression on key lifts; keep food stable unless trend is clearly wrong.

**Weeks 9–12:** same plan; deload if crushed; end with photo set + strength comparison vs week 1–2 baselines.

**End-of-phase review questions:**

- Attendance ≥80% of planned sessions?  
- Protein hit most days?  
- Key lifts progressed?  
- Photos/waist moved in the intended direction (or recomp-quiet but better shape)?  
- Keep pathway, nudge food, or switch bias for Phase 2?

## 5. YOUR TARGETS

- [ ] Programme locked 12 weeks  
- [ ] Logbook unbroken habit  
- [ ] Month 1 and month 3 photos done  
- [ ] Pathway bias executed, not fantasised  
- [ ] Review notes written before Month 4  

## 6. COMMON MISTAKES

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| New programme every fortnight | One template through month 3 |
| Ignoring protein while “training hard” | Anchor meals |
| Pathway A under-eating | Surplus check |
| Pathway C crash diet + no steps plan | Modest deficit + steps |
| Skipping deload when wrecked | Chapter 13 rule |

## 8. WHAT’S NEXT

Once the craft sticks, **Months 4–6: Build** — add productive stress: more quality volume where recovered, tighter tracking, and food adjustments that match the photos — without novelty addiction.
