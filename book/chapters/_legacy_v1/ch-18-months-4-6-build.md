# Chapter 18 — Months 4–6: Build

Foundation made you competent. Build makes you **adapt harder on purpose**. Same principles — more intelligent stress where you can recover it, cleaner tracking, fewer excuses dressed up as “optimisation.”

## 1. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

### Phase goal

Add productive training stress (usually volume or progression density), keep food honest to pathway, and use body-comp feedback without weekly panic. By month 6 you should look like someone who trains — even if you’re not “done.”

### Training upgrades (pick what you earned)

- Add **1 hard set** on lagging patterns if recovery is fine  
- Tighten RIR on work sets if you were sandbagging  
- Keep exercise selection mostly stable (progression needs continuity)  
- Optional: swap one variation if a joint hates the old one — not because TikTok said so  

If attendance or sleep collapsed in Phase 1, **fix the foundation first** — don’t pile volume onto chaos.

### Food adjustments

| Pathway | Build-phase tilt |
|---------|------------------|
| **A** | Patient surplus; if scale stuck and lifts stalled, nudge calories up before blaming genetics |
| **B** | Continue recomp/mild deficit if photos improve; if soft midsection stuck *and* lifts ok, small calorie cut or steps up |
| **C** | Protect lifts while losing; if strength free-fall, raise food slightly or slow the cut; diet break if adherence dies |
| **D** | Progressive overload honesty — stop “junk volume” theatre; match food to whether you chose gain or lose |

### Tracking quality

- Photos every 4 weeks minimum  
- Waist trend  
- Strength log on progress lifts  
- Protein days / session attendance %  

Combine signals. One soft week of water weight is not a new personality.

### Weekly template

Keep Phase 1 split unless it failed attendance. Build is usually **same skeleton, better effort, slightly more volume** — not a brand-new religion.

## 2. WHY IT MATTERS

This is where beginners either compound or restart. The ones who restart usually got bored, got soft feelings on a bulk, or got hungry on a cut and decided the programme was cursed. Build rewards people who can tolerate quiet weeks.

## 3. WHAT EXPERIENCED BODYBUILDERS DO

- Change one lever at a time.  
- Add volume only when progression and recovery support it.  
- Use diet breaks / maintenance weeks when cuts get nasty — then resume.  
- Keep cardio as servant, not master (Chapter 14).  
- Pre-commit: no programme change mid-phase unless injury or life forces MED mode.

## 4. YOUR ACTION PLAN

1. **Read your month-3 review.** Keep / nudge food / switch pathway bias.  
2. **Write Build rules:** which lifts get an extra set; what RIR target is; when you’ll deload.  
3. **Set calorie number for months 4–6** (adjust every 2–4 weeks by trend).  
4. **Schedule photos** end of months 4, 5, 6 (or at least 4 and 6).  
5. **Run weeks 13–24** with weekly reviews light, monthly reviews honest.  
6. **End of month 6 gate:** ready to Refine (aim stimulus) or extend Build if still beginner-responsive and loving progress.

## 6. COMMON MISTAKES

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Doubling volume overnight | +1 set where needed |
| New split every month | Stability |
| Ego-aborting surplus at week 8 of Build | Pre-committed block |
| Cutting into the ground while adding HIIT | Food/steps first; protect lifts |
| Ignoring sleep while “building” | Chapter 13 |
