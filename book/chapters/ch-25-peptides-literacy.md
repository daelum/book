# Chapter 25 — Peptides — What People Mean and What’s Unknown

“Peptides” in gym- Bro speech is a suitcase word. It can mean healing folklore, fat-loss experiments, growth-hormone–axis toys, or whatever a shop labelled with a science-y string of letters. Some research peptides are real medical tools in narrow contexts. A lot of consumer discourse is **C–E evidence** wearing a lab coat.

Same book rules: **literacy, not protocols.** No unsupervised beginner recommendations. No dosing schedules. Legality and quality control vary wildly — grey-market vials are not pharmacies.

## 1. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

### What a peptide is (plain English)

Short chains of amino acids that can signal biology. Insulin is a peptide hormone. That doesn’t mean every Instagram peptide is insulin-level understood.

### Why the category feels confusing

- Different compounds, different targets, different risk profiles  
- Human evidence often thin, early, or extrapolated from animals  
- Compounding / research-chemical markets ≠ regulated medication pathways  
- Marketing outruns monitoring  
- Affiliate culture sells certainty (kickbacks don’t equal clinical trials)

### Buckets you’ll hear about (names for literacy, not a menu)

| Bucket | What people claim | Evidence vibe (honest) |
|--------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Injury / recovery folklore | Faster healing | Widely marketed “healing” peptides (e.g. BPC-157 talk) have abundant preclinical claims but **very low-certainty human** evidence — preclinical ≠ protocol; medical rehab still rules. *(Educational claim `clm_30a9915f28ab`; bib `bib_f9a28d90c7`, `bib_a25fc70d86`.)* |
| GH-axis secretagogues | Sleep, recovery, body comp | Mixed/limited; age and context matter; not free |
| Metabolic / fat-loss peptides | Appetite or fat mobilisation stories | Overlaps marketing; some agents are drugs with real effects and real risks |
| “Kitchen sink” blends | Everything | Usually a hard pass for serious thinking |

If someone recommends a stack because they have a discount code, that’s commerce — mark it **E** for incentives even before you mark the biology.

### Unknowns that matter

- Long-term safety in healthy lifters  
- Contaminants and mislabelling in unregulated supply  
- Interaction with existing meds/conditions  
- Whether “feeling recovered” is biology or placebo + better sleep that week  

**Curiosity ≠ protocol.** Wanting to understand is fine. Treating a podcast as a prescription is how people become cautionary tales.

### SARMs and “beginner PED” marketing (adjacent literacy)

You’ll also hear selective androgen receptor modulators pitched as softer on-ramps. Framing something as “beginner-friendly” is marketing language, not a risk waiver. Many of these agents still sit in grey legal/regulatory space depending on jurisdiction, with incomplete long-term data. Same rule: not a Year-1 action plan item in this book.

## 2. WHY IT MATTERS

Peptides are where sceptical curiosity and hype collide. If you can’t name evidence class, you’ll get sold. If you dismiss all of modern endocrinology as fake, you’ll miss real medicine. The skilled move is **precision**: what molecule, what claim, what evidence, what risk, what legal pathway.

## 3. WHAT EXPERIENCED BODYBUILDERS DO (OBSERVATION)

- They separate “interesting paper” from “I should inject this Thursday.”  
- They notice lifestyle (sleep, food, progressive training) still explains a lot of recovery — medication isn’t the whole story.  
- They treat grey-market sourcing as a risk factor by itself.  
- They don’t let peptide rabbit holes replace getting strong.

## 4. YOUR ACTION PLAN (DEFAULTS)

1. **Keep Year-1 physique plan drug-free** (including research peptides).  
2. **When you see a claim:** force the sentence: evidence class A–F + “compared to hitting protein and sleep.”  
3. **Medical symptoms / injuries:** see clinicians and physios — not peptide catalogues.  
4. **If a clinician ever discusses a legitimate peptide medication:** that’s a medical lane, not a gym protocol from this book.  
5. **Ignore discount-code science.**
