# Stage 5A — The Twelve-Month Follow-Along Playbook

**The promise:** run The STRIPLINE Method for twelve months — strip fat first, lock one plan, lift progressively, link to accountability — and you give yourself a real shot at the best physique of your life from wherever you are starting. Not a six-week miracle. Not a bulk that ignores the midsection. A year of honest sequence with specific tools, templates, and decision rules.

This chapter is the follow-along operating system. You should be able to open it in Month 1 and still be using it in Month 12 without inventing a new religion every Sunday night.

If you still want a human in the loop later, that door sits here as a soft next step — **[PLACEHOLDER: paid offer / coach funnel — pending agreement]** — without replacing the plan in your hands.

## How Stage 5 splits (read this once, then execute)

Stage 5 is too important to bury inside one overloaded pamphlet. It splits into three working chapters plus a later logbook appendix:

| Part | Job | What you do with it |
|------|-----|---------------------|
| **5A (this chapter)** | The twelve-month journey | Scoreboard, Week 0/1 setup, month-by-month actions, reviews, plateaus, chaos SOPs |
| **5B** | Nutrition system | Calories, macros, tracking workflow, defaults for busy days |
| **5C** | Training system | Default **PPL** and **PPLU** programmes, effort rules, deloads, logging |
| **Year Logbook (later appendix)** | Write-in pages | Weekly/monthly checklists, waist/photo trackers, training and food logs for the full year |

**Rule of use:** 5A tells you *what month you’re in and what “good” looks like*. 5B sets the food numbers. 5C sets the sessions. The Year Logbook is where you write the receipts.

Metering tool for food targets (also covered in 5B): **https://yehbro.com/food-bro/**

Keep Stage 2’s definitions in your head: **STRIP → LINE → LIFT → LINK**. This chapter installs them on a calendar.

## How to use this chapter across the year

1. **Week 0–1:** complete the setup checklists; do not skip photos.
2. **Every training week:** run floors + LINK template; log food and lifts.
3. **Every 4 weeks:** run the review script; apply the one-lever tree only if needed.
4. **At month boundaries:** read that month’s section the weekend before it starts; write the “next action” in your calendar.
5. **At Months 3, 6, 9, 12:** use the block tables as a dashboard; complete photo comparisons.
6. **Whenever life explodes:** open chaos SOPs first — not a new PDF.

You should not need to re-read Stage 1–4 every month. You *should* reopen the month you are in, the review script, and Stages 5B/5C when numbers or sessions need detail.

## Week 0 / Week 1 setup checklist

Do not “start training harder” before you can measure whether anything changed. Week 0 is setup. Week 1 is first honest execution.

### Week 0 — baseline and lock (exact steps)

Complete every box before you chase clever programming.

**1. Photos (same conditions)**
- [ ] Front, side, back — relaxed posture
- [ ] Same bathroom/light if possible; same time of day (morning after bathroom is best)
- [ ] No pump-flex Olympics for the baseline set (optional pumped shots can be extra — keep one standard set forever)
- [ ] Save dated files somewhere you will still find in twelve months

**2. Waist**
- [ ] Tape at navel, relaxed, consistent tension
- [ ] Record number + date in workbook / notes

**3. Scale protocol (supporting actor, not judge)**
- [ ] Weigh most mornings after bathroom, before food/drink
- [ ] Plan to average the week — single days are noise

**4. Food Bro PDF**
- [ ] Enter stats at **https://yehbro.com/food-bro/**
- [ ] Download free tailored PDF (cut / maintain / lean bulk as relevant)
- [ ] Paste calories + protein into workbook and your food app goals

**5. Apps (pick one each — stop shopping)**
- [ ] Food logging: Cronometer (primary default) *or* MyFitnessPal *or* MacroFactor — see Stage 5B
- [ ] Training logging: Hevy (primary default) *or* Strong *or* a notebook that you will actually open — see Stage 5C
- [ ] Steps: whatever watch/phone you already own; set a floor number

**6. Pick the programme (Stage 5C)**
- [ ] **PPL** if you can honestly guard ~6 training days most weeks
- [ ] **PPLU** if **4 hard days** is the real life version
- [ ] **3-day full body** only if 4+ days is fantasy this season
- [ ] Write the days on the calendar for the next 8–12 weeks — no redesign loop

**7. LINE lock (write these once)**
- [ ] Calorie target + protein target (from Food Bro + 5B)
- [ ] Step floor (STRIP default often 7–10k+; pick a number you can hit on workdays)
- [ ] Sleep floor (defend a minimum — many adults need to fight for 7+)
- [ ] Deload rule (e.g. every 6–8 weeks, or when sleep + joints scream)
- [ ] Review date every 4 weeks (put it in the calendar now)
- [ ] LINK person: coach / mate / partner who can ask “did you follow the written plan?” — or soft door **[PLACEHOLDER: paid offer / coach funnel — pending agreement]**

**8. Starting-point call (one sentence)**
- [ ] Higher body fat → STRIP primary
- [ ] Skinny-fat → STRIP/recomp; no dirty bulk escape
- [ ] Already lean hardgainer → complete the exception checklist later in this chapter
- [ ] Restarter → audit, simplify to one template, then STRIP/LIFT from photos

**Week 0 exit rule:** if photos, waist, Food Bro targets, programme choice, and floors are not written, you are still in Week 0. Gym heroics without a baseline are just expensive cardio cosplay.

### Week 1 — first honest week (exact steps)

- [ ] Train every scheduled day on the locked template (MED version only if life explodes — see chaos SOPs)
- [ ] Log every work set before you leave the gym or close the app
- [ ] Hit protein daily; hit calories as a weekly average (perfect days optional; averages mandatory)
- [ ] Hit step floor ≥5 of 7 days
- [ ] Sleep floor defended at least on training nights
- [ ] One mid-week LINK check-in: sessions done / protein hit? / one sentence on energy
- [ ] Do **not** change the programme, the calorie target, or the app stack mid-week

**Week 1 exit rule:** attendance + logging + protein happened. Looking transformed by Sunday is not the exit rule.

## Scoreboard definitions

If the scale is your only judge, you will make stupid decisions. Scale weight includes fat, muscle, water, gut content, glycogen, salt, stress hormones, and “I trained legs yesterday.”

### Primary scoreboard (decide with these)

| Metric | How to take it | Cadence | What it tells you |
|--------|----------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Photos** | Same poses, light, time; front/side/back | Every 2–4 weeks | Shape, midsection honesty, limb fullness |
| **Waist** | Navel, relaxed, consistent tension | Weekly (same day) + every review | Fat trajectory on the midsection |
| **Strength** | Logbook on main lifts (load × reps × sets) | Every session | Muscle signal is alive or dying |
| **Adherence** | Sessions hit / protein days / step days / sleep nights | Weekly score out of simple totals | Whether you ran the plan |

### Secondary scoreboard (useful, not sacred)

- Trend weight (weekly averages)
- How clothes fit (belt notch, shirt sleeves, jeans waist)
- Energy / hunger / mood notes (one line)
- Joint feel (not hypochondria — early warning)

### How to read the board

| Photos | Waist | Strength | Verdict |
|--------|-------|----------|---------|
| Improving | Down or stable-down | Holding or rising | Winning — stay the course |
| Soft / worse | Up | Rising | Likely surplus too big or steps collapsed — one lever |
| “Smaller” but soft | Down fast | Collapsing | Shrink mode — eat up / recover; you are not transforming |
| Flat | Flat | Flat | Check adherence first; then plateau tree |

**Adherence score (simple weekly template):**

- Training days planned: __ / hit: __
- Protein days (≥90% of target): __ / 7
- Step-floor days: __ / 7
- Sleep-floor nights: __ / 7

If adherence is junk, you do not have a mysterious plateau. You have a compliance problem. Fix the week before you rewrite the physics.

## Tools stack (defaults you can swap later)

Pick tools once in Week 0. Consistency beats brand loyalty contests.

### Food tracking apps

| Role | Default | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| Primary | **Cronometer** | Accurate database; clean enough for beginners who weigh food |
| Strong alternative | **MyFitnessPal** | Huge barcode coverage (handy in AU shops) — verify entries |
| Paid coaching-style option | **MacroFactor** | Useful if you want algorithm-assisted adjustments and will pay |

**Rule:** one app for ≥8 weeks. Verify barcodes. Log oils and cooking fats. Review **weekly averages**, not single-meal purity.

Pair with Food Bro: set targets at **https://yehbro.com/food-bro/** → enter the same calories/protein as app goals → log daily → bring weekly averages into the 4-week review.

### Training log apps

| Role | Default | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| Primary | **Hevy** | Clean templates, rest timers, history you will actually check |
| Strong iOS-first alternative | **Strong** | Excellent session UX if you live on iPhone |
| Offline fallback | Notebook / Notes app | Valid if you open it every session |

**Rule:** if it is not logged, it did not progress — you exercised. Stage 5C gives the PPL/PPLU sessions to load.

### Steps / wearables
Use whatever you already own. The number that matters is the **floor you hit**, not the brand on your wrist. Recalibrate if a new watch reads differently — compare trends inside one device.

### Workbook / Year Logbook
Use the companion workbook sheets now; the full Year Logbook appendix later is the print-friendly twelve-month home for checklists, waist/photo trackers, and weekly reviews. Until that appendix is in your hands, a notes doc with dated headings is enough — as long as Month 12 can find Month 1.

## Floors, defaults, and the weekly operating rhythm

The year fails in the gaps between “knowing” and “running.” These are the defaults that keep STRIPLINE alive when motivation is average.

### Non-negotiable floors (write numbers)

| Floor | How to choose | Example starting defaults |
|-------|---------------|---------------------------|
| **Protein** | From Food Bro / Stage 5B (~1.6–2.2 g/kg practical band) | e.g. 160–200 g depending on size/goal weight |
| **Calories** | Phase-appropriate average (STRIP deficit; LIFT small surplus when earned) | Paste Food Bro cut/maintain/lean-bulk figure |
| **Steps** | High enough to matter in STRIP; sustainable on workdays | Often 7–10k+; raise before savage calorie cuts |
| **Sleep** | Minimum you will defend most nights | Many adults: fight for 7+; training nights first |
| **Training days** | From Stage 5C choice | 6 (PPL), 4 (PPLU), or 3 (full body) |
| **LINK touchpoints** | Weekly at minimum | Same weekday; three lines is enough |

If a floor is fantasy relative to your roster, lower it to something hittable and raise it later. A fake 12k step target you miss daily is worse than an 8k floor you own.

### Default week shape (ordinary adult)

**Sunday (10–15 min):** glance at written sessions and food defaults. No redesign. Note travel/overtime. Confirm LINK check-in day.

**Training days:** compounds first, log before you leave, protein planned around the session without perfection theatre.

**Rest / step days:** step floor still counts. Active recovery walks beat “rest means sofa and delivery chaos” for most people in STRIP.

**Friday or Saturday:** social meal rule already written — estimate, protein-first where possible, weekly average still trends correctly.

**Review Friday every fourth week:** full script. Other weeks: adherence score only.

### Starting-point modifiers (bias inside one method)

| Starting reality | Month 1–3 bias | Watch-outs |
|------------------|----------------|------------|
| Higher body fat | Clear STRIP deficit; steps matter; lift like muscle matters | Crash diets; cardio that kills lifting |
| Skinny-fat | Mild deficit or recomp; high protein; no dirty bulk escape | Feeling “small” and bulking into softness |
| Lean hardgainer (documented) | Short stop-undereating → patient surplus | Denial soft bulk; skipping waist checks |
| Restarter with history | Audit, simplify one template, then STRIP/LIFT from photos | Novelty addiction; ego loading on a cut |

These are modifiers, not four different books.

### Worked example — reading a noisy month

**Sam, Month 2:** scale up 0.6 kg week-to-week after a salty restaurant weekend. Panic says “reverse diet” or “new programme.” Scoreboard says: waist −1 cm across four weeks, side photo tighter, bench holding, adherence 6/7 protein days. **Decision:** stay course. The scale told a salt story. The waist and photos told the fat story.

**Jordan, Month 5:** scale down, lifts down hard, photos “smaller soft,” sleep 5–6 hours. Panic says “cut harder.” Scoreboard says shrink mode. **Decision:** raise calories ~150–200, defend sleep, MED volume one week, keep steps. Fat loss that deletes training is not the product.

Use examples like these in your own reviews. Story first from photos/waist/strength/adherence — then the scale as supporting cast.

## Month-by-month follow-along (Months 1–12)

Each month below uses the same five blocks: phase bias, what to do, what to track, what “good” looks like, failure modes, next action. Run the month you are in. Do not live in Month 12 fantasy during Month 2.

### Month 1 — Install the craft (STRIP + LINE)

**STRIPLINE phase bias:** STRIP + LINE. Fat-loss-first for anyone with meaningful fat or skinny-fat softness. Hardgainer exception only if documented.

**What to do this month**
- Run Week 0 / Week 1 checklists completely.
- Lock PPL or PPLU (or 3-day) and stop shopping programmes.
- Practise Food Bro targets with real logging — oils, sauces, “bites,” alcohol included.
- Learn hard-enough sets without form collapse (Stage 5C effort rules).
- Build default meals you can repeat on workdays (Stage 5B).
- Install LINK: weekly check-in habit, even if it is short and boring.

**What to track**
- Photos (Week 0 + end of Month 1)
- Waist weekly
- Weekly weight average
- Session log every training day
- Adherence score weekly
- Hunger/energy one-liner 2–3×/week

**What “good” looks like**
- You can explain your calorie + protein targets without vague vibes.
- ≥80% of planned sessions happened.
- Protein hit most days.
- Midsection trajectory started *or* exception written with a review date.
- Logbook exists and is ugly in a useful way.

**Common failure modes**
- Skipping photos “until I look better.”
- Changing the split three times because a mate runs something cooler.
- Tracking food Monday–Thursday, disappearing Friday–Sunday.
- Judging the whole year by a noisy scale on day nine.
- Going so hard on cardio that lifting and sleep die.

**Exact weekly checklist (Month 1)**
- Mon–Sun: hit scheduled sessions or MED substitute
- Daily: protein target; log food same day
- Daily: steps toward floor
- 1× LINK: sessions / protein / energy
- End of week: adherence score + weekly weight average

**Next action into Month 2**
Keep the same template. Do a short review: waist trend, strength on 3 main lifts, adherence average. Adjust **one** lever only if needed (usually steps or a small calorie tweak). No new programme.

---

### Month 2 — Make the boring reliable (STRIP + LINE)

**STRIPLINE phase bias:** STRIP + LINE. Habit reliability over novelty.

**What to do this month**
- Automate groceries and default breakfasts/lunches.
- Film one work set per major lift this month (form check).
- Protect sleep floor like it is a training appointment.
- Practise social-meal rules written in advance (estimate, budget, return next meal).
- If travel/shift chaos hits, use chaos SOPs — do not invent a temporary religion.

**What to track**
- Same primary scoreboard
- Note which meals are “autopilot” vs improvisation
- Step average vs floor
- Any missed LINK check-ins (fix them; do not mythologise)

**What “good” looks like**
- Weekends no longer erase weekdays every time.
- You know your working weights on main lifts.
- Hunger is managed with plan tools (protein, volume foods, sleep), not with “new diet” shopping.
- Waist trending favourably *or* recomp signs (tighter photos, held strength) if skinny-fat.

**Common failure modes**
- “Clean eating” without logging because tracking felt tedious for five days.
- Adding random HIIT because fat loss feels slow at week six.
- Ego loading while dieting; joints complain; you call it “bad genetics.”
- Secret surplus via coffee-shop extras and “I deserved it” dinners.

**Decision rules unique to Month 2**
- If weekends erase weekdays: pre-log a default Saturday intake and a planned social meal budget — do not “wing it and regret.”
- If lifts feel ugly: film sets before adding load; reduce range only if pain demands a listed alternate from Stage 5C.
- If hunger is loud but waist is moving: keep protein high, favour volume foods (Stage 5B), sleep harder — do not panic-eat a new diet identity.

**Next action into Month 3**
Run a proper 4-week review script (later in this chapter). Decide: stay STRIP, lighten deficit slightly if strength is dying, or hold course. Still no programme hop.

---

### Month 3 — Prove the system under real life (STRIP + LINE)

**STRIPLINE phase bias:** STRIP + LINE. Exit foundation with receipts, not feelings.

**What to do this month**
- Stress-test the plan against overtime, kids’ sport, social season — using MED weeks when needed.
- Nail double progression: top of rep range with good form → add load.
- Confirm deload timing if joints/sleep are fraying.
- Compare Month 1 photos to Month 3 before declaring anything cursed.
- Write a one-paragraph “what actually works for my schedule” note.

**What to track**
- Full Month 3 photo set vs Month 1
- Waist vs Month 1
- Strength on the same 3–5 lifts
- Adherence average across Months 1–3
- How many MED weeks you needed (data, not shame)

**What “good” looks like**
- Clear midsection improvement **or** documented hardgainer exception still valid on review.
- Strength mostly held or improved on key lifts.
- You trust the process enough to stop collecting PDFs.
- LINK still happening without drama.

**Common failure modes**
- Boredom bulk: “I’ve been good; time to eat big,” while photos still soft.
- Quitting because a stranger on social media looks better after “8 weeks” (unknown drugs, lighting, years of training).
- Perfect weekdays + obliterated weekends = mysterious plateau cosplay.
- Delaying review because you “already know” — memory lies.

**Month 3 exit interview (write answers)**
1. What broke most often — training, food logging, steps, sleep, or weekends?
2. What default fixed it when I used it?
3. Midsection vs Month 1: better / same / worse?
4. Am I bored of a working plan and tempted to hop? (If yes, that is a warning light.)
5. Phase decision for Months 4–6 in one sentence:

**Next action into Month 4**
Block decision: continue STRIP, shift to recomp/hold, or begin early LIFT only if STRIP→LIFT checklist passes. Write the decision in one sentence.

### Month 4 — Productive stress without chaos (STRIP continues or transition)

**STRIPLINE phase bias:** STRIP continues for most. Transition only if criteria earned.

**What to do this month**
- Keep the same programme skeleton (Stage 5C). Add at most one accessory or one set where a weak point is honest — not a new identity.
- If still stripping: keep deficit sustainable; raise steps before nuking calories into misery.
- If transitioning: nudge calories toward maintenance first for 1–2 weeks, then small surplus if photos stay honest.
- Rehearse recovery: sleep, deload rule, stress management on purpose.

**What to track**
- Photos every 2–4 weeks
- Waist response to any calorie change within 2–3 weeks
- Session quality (RIR honesty)
- Adherence under slightly higher life load (common in this block)

**What “good” looks like**
- You can train hard without treating every session like a funeral.
- Fat trajectory still honest if STRIP continues.
- If calories rose, waist did not sprint away while lifts stayed lazy.
- Plan still written; mood is not the programmer.

**Common failure modes**
- “Transition” that is actually untracked eating.
- Adding volume because you feel flat, while sleep is wrecked.
- Comparing to Month 12 goals and panicking into extremes.
- Dropping LINK because “I’ve got this now.”

**Transition protocol (only if earned)**
1. Move from deficit → estimated maintenance for 7–14 days.
2. Keep protein and training identical.
3. Watch waist + photos.
4. If midsection stays honest, add ~100–200 kcal for a small surplus (Stage 5B).
5. If waist jumps and photos soften, return to STRIP. No debate club.

**Next action into Month 5**
Confirm phase in writing again. If waist climbed faster than strength/limbs after a surplus nudge, return to STRIP without a speech.

### Month 5 — Hold the line when motivation thins (STRIP / early LIFT)

**STRIPLINE phase bias:** STRIP for soft midsections; early LIFT only if earned.

**What to do this month**
- Expect motivation to dip. Systems carry you: defaults, calendar, LINK.
- Audit logging quality (verify barcodes, weigh oils, stop “eyeballing the peanut butter”).
- Push progressive overload where recovery allows; take the deload if earned.
- Practise restaurant estimation without abandoning the weekly average.

**What to track**
- Adherence especially (this is the month people quietly quit logging)
- Waist + photos
- Sleep vs training performance correlation
- Alcohol / social frequency vs weekly calorie average

**What “good” looks like**
- You train on days you do not “feel like a bodybuilder.”
- Food logging is boring and mostly accurate.
- Physique is clearly different from Month 1 in side-by-side photos.
- You are not shopping for a new method because a plateau week happened.

**Common failure modes**
- Ghosting the food app while still “kind of dieting.”
- Revenge workouts after a bad weekend.
- Supplement stacking as a substitute for steps and protein.
- Using “maintenance break” as a euphemism for chaos.

**Motivation-dip protocol**
- Shrink decisions: same breakfast, same gym start time, same LINK weekday.
- Use MED sessions rather than zeros when life spikes.
- Re-read Month 1 photos before you declare the plan dead.
- Do not open a new programme PDF as entertainment.

**Next action into Month 6**
Run 4-week review. Decide whether Months 6–7 stay STRIP or officially enter LIFT emphasis. Use the checklist, not boredom.

### Month 6 — Mid-year audit (STRIP finish or LIFT gate)

**STRIPLINE phase bias:** Mid-year fork — continue STRIP with integrity **or** enter LIFT if criteria met.

**What to do this month**
- Full mid-year photo set (same conditions as Month 1).
- Complete STRIP→LIFT criteria checklist (below).
- If staying in STRIP: refresh defaults, tighten weekend leaks, keep lifting progressive.
- If entering LIFT: raise food with intent (Stage 5B surplus defaults), keep the same template, watch waist weekly.
- Update Year Logbook pages so Months 7–12 are not improvisation.

**What to track**
- Month 1 vs Month 6 photos (mandatory)
- Waist delta
- Strength delta on main lifts
- Adherence average Months 4–6
- Written phase decision for Months 7–9

**What “good” looks like**
- Honest call: keep stripping **or** build — based on midsection evidence.
- Strength story is mostly protected across the first half.
- You know your failure patterns and have countermeasures written.
- LINK still on the calendar.

**Common failure modes**
- Dirty bulk celebration because “half the year is done.”
- Cutting harder out of impatience when adherence was the real issue.
- Changing to an advanced split to “mark the new phase.”
- Skipping the mid-year photo comparison.

**Fork sentence (copy):** “For Months 7–9 I will run ______ because photos/waist/adherence show ______.”

**Next action into Month 7**
Execute the written fork. If LIFT: surplus is small and tracked. If STRIP: no self-pity — integrity compounds.

### Month 7 — LIFT emphasis when earned (or STRIP integrity)

**STRIPLINE phase bias:** LIFT emphasis **if** Month 6 checklist passed; otherwise STRIP continues (no drama).

**What to do this month (LIFT path)**
- Calories at small surplus or strong maintenance with high protein.
- Chase overload: reps, load, control — logged.
- Keep steps from collapsing to zero; NEAT often rises in surplus, but do not invent a sofa bulk.
- Add weak-point work sparingly (1–2 movements), not a new programme brand.

**What to do this month (STRIP path)**
- Same as earlier STRIP months: deficit honesty, protein, progressive training, steps.
- Ignore calendar envy from people who earned LIFT earlier. Your photos are the referee.

**What to track**
- Waist weekly (LIFT path: early warning)
- Strength progression rate
- Weight trend vs photo look
- Recovery (sleep, resting feel, joint notes)

**What “good” looks like**
- LIFT: limbs filling, strength rising, waist not sprinting.
- STRIP: continued midsection progress without strength freefall.
- Either path: adherence still adult.

**Common failure modes**
- LIFT becoming weekend buffet + weekday denial.
- STRIP lifters “rewarding” themselves into a soft surplus accidentally.
- Volume explosion because surplus energy feels motivating.
- Dropping cardio/steps to zero and calling it recovery.

**LIFT weekly guardrails**
- Surplus size stays small enough that weekly waist change is slow, not dramatic.
- Protein remains non-negotiable (surplus carbs do not replace it).
- Progression logged every session — “I ate more” is not overload.
- If clothes tighten at the waist faster than sleeves: trim food or raise steps before you invent a cut panic.

**STRIP weekly guardrails (if still here)**
- Deficit still trainable.
- Compare to *your* Month 1, not to people who earned LIFT earlier.
- Raise steps or tighten logging before another aggressive calorie slash.

**Next action into Month 8**
Keep the path. Mini-review at week 4. One lever only if waist or strength misbehaves.

### Month 8 — Build skill under load (LIFT / continued STRIP)

**STRIPLINE phase bias:** Same as Month 7 — stay consistent inside the chosen bias.

**What to do this month**
- Double down on form under heavier loads; film a set again.
- Schedule the deload if you skipped it in the last 6–8 hard weeks.
- Practise high-protein surplus meals that are not pure junk (Stage 5B).
- If still stripping, consider a brief diet break at maintenance if adherence is dying — planned, timed, not a free-for-all.

**What to track**
- Quality of last reps (are they still the target muscle?)
- Waist vs strength (LIFT)
- Hunger vs sleep (STRIP)
- Missed sessions causes (schedule vs motivation vs illness)

**What “good” looks like**
- Progression continues without weekly injury scares.
- Body looks more athletic than Month 1 even if scale story is mixed.
- You can explain why you are still in STRIP or LIFT in one calm sentence.

**Common failure modes**
- Ignoring nagging pain until it becomes a month off.
- Programme hop disguised as “weak point specialisation camp.”
- Comparing enhanced timelines to your natural Month 8.
- Letting LINK die because results feel “fine enough.”

**Deload reminder**
If you have run 6–8 hard weeks without an easier week, take one: reduce set count and/or load, keep frequency, sleep harder. Coming back stronger beats collecting a mystery shoulder injury as a personality.

**Pain rule:** sharp joint pain that alters mechanics → swap to the listed alternate (Stage 5C) and reduce load. Soreness from hard training is not the same thing. If unsure, MED and ask a qualified professional — do not ego through structural pain.

**Next action into Month 9**
Block review: are you still on the right side of the STRIP/LIFT gate? Adjust food or steps one notch if needed. Keep the skeleton.

## Plateau one-lever tree

**Definition of a real plateau:** adherence is genuinely high for ~2–4 weeks **and** photos/waist/strength are stuck or moving the wrong way.

If adherence is low, it is not a plateau. Fix compliance.

### Tree (change one lever, then re-review in 2–4 weeks)

1. **Steps / NEAT**  
   Raise daily step floor by ~1–2k (STRIP) or restore a collapsed floor (any phase). Silent NEAT drops sabotage deficits.

2. **Calories (small)**  
   STRIP stalled fat loss → trim ~100–200 kcal/day **or** tighten weekend logging accuracy first if leaks are obvious.  
   Strength collapsing / misery → raise ~100–200 kcal.  
   LIFT waist sprinting → trim surplus; do not “train more” as the first lie.

3. **Sleep / stress load**  
   Defend sleep floor; cut junk volume; use MED sessions for 1 week if life is on fire.

4. **Training honesty**  
   Are work sets actually near failure with decent form? Is progression logged? Add **one** hard set to a lagging lift **or** clean up RIR — not both plus a new split.

5. **Only then consider structure**  
   Swap an exercise for a pain-free alternate; deload; or change days to match the calendar. Programme-hop as first response is how you stay stuck with better branding.

**Hard rule:** one lever. Write it down. Give it time. Then review.

### Worked plateau cases

**Case A — fake plateau:** “Nothing’s happening.” Digging shows 3/7 protein days and two skipped sessions. **Action:** MED week to reset habits; no calorie change; no new split.

**Case B — STRIP stall with good adherence:** waist flat 3 weeks, photos unchanged, lifts holding, steps drifted from 9k → 5k. **Action:** restore step floor first. Re-review in 3 weeks.

**Case C — STRIP stall, steps good:** logging verified, steps solid, waist flat, mild strength rise. **Action:** trim ~150 kcal/day. Hold programme.

**Case D — LIFT waist creep:** surplus on paper looks small, sleeves improving, waist +2 cm in a month, weekend logging sparse. **Action:** fix weekend logging accuracy for 2 weeks before cutting food; if still climbing, trim surplus.

**Case E — strength crash on cut:** photos improving but every lift down and sleep poor. **Action:** raise calories, sleep floor, optional 1-week volume MED — not “add more intensity techniques.”

### What not to do when stalled
- Stack five changes on Monday.
- Start a 14-day detox.
- Copy an advanced bodybuilder’s volume.
- Throw away twelve weeks of data because week thirteen was annoying.

## Chaos / travel / sick SOPs

Life will try to delete your plan. Pre-decide the minimum so Thursday-you cannot invent ethics in a hotel bar.

### Bad-week MED plan (still “on programme”)

| Lever | Minimum effective dose |
|-------|------------------------|
| Training | Stage 5C MED sessions: key compounds only, shorter time |
| Protein | Hit the gram target even if food is boring / repetitive |
| Calories | Keep the weekly average intent; do not “save” then binge |
| Steps | Floor, not fantasy — walks count |
| Sleep | Protect the minimum on as many nights as possible |
| Forbidden | Inventing a new split at 11 p.m.; starting a detox; zeroing the plan until “next Monday” |

### Travel SOP
- [ ] Before you leave: know hotel/gym access or bodyweight/DB alternatives from Stage 5C
- [ ] Protein defaults packed or located (bars, sachets, shops near the site)
- [ ] Step floor stays; sightseeing walks are training assistants
- [ ] Log in the app the same day — memory fails on trips
- [ ] Social meals: estimate, pick protein-first plates, return to plan next meal
- [ ] If training days must drop: hit 2 quality sessions rather than 0, then resume home template without rewrite

### Sick SOP
- [ ] If febrile / acute illness: train less or not at all; sleep; protein as appetite allows; drop the hero story
- [ ] Resume with MED sessions for several days — do not PR the first day back
- [ ] Do not “make up” all missed sessions in 72 hours
- [ ] Extend the phase by the sick days; do not panic-cut to compensate
- [ ] If illness lasts >1–2 weeks, redo a mini Week 0 (waist/photos optional refresh) and continue

### Overtime / kids / shift-work SOP
- [ ] Calendar the sessions first; accessories are optional, compounds are not
- [ ] Default meals beat inspiration
- [ ] LINK check-in can be three lines on your phone
- [ ] Use PPLU or 3-day if PPL is fantasy this roster cycle — that is LINE intelligence, not quitting

### Fatherhood + brutal hours (the real constraint story)

STRIPLINE was written for people who do **not** have unlimited gym time. Stage 4’s why-me is blunt on purpose: transformation under **fatherhood** and **~70-hour construction weeks** — not a content-creator calendar. If your week looks like site/ overtime/ school runs/ bedtime negotiations, you are not behind the method. You are the method’s intended user.

What survives that kind of week:

- **Locked sessions that fit the calendar** — early, after shift, or lunch. Duration can shrink; the written day does not vanish.
- **Protein defaults** that don’t need a perfect kitchen every night — same breakfast, packed lunch, boring dinner that still hits grams.
- **LINK that still asks** when Thursday-you wants to negotiate — a mate or the soft coaching door when it opens: **[PLACEHOLDER: paid offer / coach funnel — pending agreement]**.
- **MED before zero** — a shortened compounds-only session keeps the identity alive after a twelve-hour day; a cancelled month does not.

What dies on that calendar: programme-hopping, chef-level meal prep as a personality, and “I’ll start when life calms down.” Life rarely calms down. LINE is how you train inside the noise.
