# Chapter 16 — Choosing Your Pathway (A–D)

Part One gave you the map. Part Two gave you the tools. Part Three is the year. First decision: **which pathway are you actually on** — not the one that flatters you, the one the mirror and logbook support.

You can switch after Phase 1. You shouldn’t switch every Thursday because a reel hurt your feelings.

## 1. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

### The four pathways (recap)

| ID | You look / feel like… | First-year bias |
|----|------------------------|-----------------|
| **A** | Light frame, low muscle, hard to eat | Surplus + progressive lifting |
| **B** | Soft middle, soft limbs (“skinny-fat”) | Recomp or mild deficit + hard training + protein |
| **C** | Meaningful fat to lose | Sustainable deficit + muscle retention |
| **D** | Been around gyms / sports; inconsistent results | Audit & simplify before novelty |

### Decision tree (honest version)

1. **Photos from Chapter 6 in front of you.** Not memory. Photos.  
2. **If body fat is clearly high and health/comfort demand loss → C** (or B if you’re smaller-framed but soft).  
3. **If you’re light and struggle to gain weight → A.**  
4. **If you’re soft without much muscle → B.**  
5. **If you’ve trained before and keep restarting → D**, then layer A/B/C nutrition bias underneath.

Secondary lens (not a separate book): fat-loss focused vs muscle-gain focused vs curious about advanced tools. Year-one **action plans stay drug-free**; curiosity waits for Part Four literacy.

### Switching rules

- Run your pathway for **Months 1–3** before a major rewrite.  
- Switch when photos + waist + strength say the bias is wrong — not when motivation is bored.  
- Hybrid is allowed: “D restart logistics + C calorie bias.”

## 2. WHY IT MATTERS

Wrong pathway wastes months. Skinny blokes cutting. Overweight beginners dirty-bulking. Restarters collecting advanced techniques with no protein target. The pathway is a **bias for decisions**, not a prison tattoo.

## 3. WHAT EXPERIENCED BODYBUILDERS DO

- **Pick a phase goal and pre-commit a block length.**  
- **Match food to the look they want next**, not to ego.  
- **Reassess on schedule** (end of Phase 1).  
- **Keep training principles constant** when nutrition bias changes.

## 4. YOUR ACTION PLAN

1. Open baseline photos + waist + strength notes.  
2. Write one sentence: “I’m pathway __ because __.”  
3. Write nutrition bias: surplus / maintenance-recomp / deficit.  
4. Confirm training frequency from Part Two still fits.  
5. Set Phase 1 review date (end of month 3).  
6. Tell your accountability human the pathway in one line.

## 6. COMMON MISTAKES

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Choosing A because “bulk sounds cool” while carrying high fat | Use photos; pick C/B |
| Eternal recomp with no progressive training | Recomp still needs overload |
| Changing pathway weekly | Phase blocks |
| Advanced-tool curiosity hijacking year one | Literacy later; fundamentals now |
