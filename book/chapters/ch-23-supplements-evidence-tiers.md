# Chapter 23 — Supplements: What Is Worth It

Most tubs are hope with a scoop. A few earn their place. Learn the difference and you’ll stop funding someone else’s boat while your logbook stays empty.

Food, progressive training, and sleep still do more than almost any powder. Supplements are the easy end of the tools conversation — useful when they plug a real gap, cosplay when they replace the work.

## 1. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

### Evidence shorthand

| Class | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **A–B** | Strong / moderate support for a clear effect |
| **C** | Emerging or limited |
| **D** | Coach/practical consensus without airtight trials |
| **E** | Anecdote |
| **F** | Marketing cosplay |

### Tier 1 — usually worth it once training and protein exist

| Supplement | Why | Practical notes |
|------------|-----|-----------------|
| **Creatine monohydrate** | Strength, power, lean-mass support | Best-supported performance supplement in healthy users at studied intakes. Daily use beats timing theatre. Loading optional. Prefer plain monohydrate. *(Claim `clm_35b78a69d71c`; bib `bib_06b671ef36`, `bib_0ad83801ce`.)* |
| **Protein powder** | Hit totals on busy days | Convenience, not magic vs food when protein is matched. *(Claim `clm_808a8b7a439d`.)* |
| **Caffeine** | Focus and training output | Works for many people — don’t nuke sleep or run anxiety through the roof. *(Claim `clm_b8f879e58769`; bib `bib_deb598f2ee`.)* |

### Soft health add-ons (not physique cheat codes)

| Supplement | When it’s sensible |
|------------|--------------------|
| **Vitamin D** | Low sun / confirmed low labs — bloodwork beats guessing |
| **Fish oil / EPA+DHA** | If fatty fish is rare in your diet |

### Tier 2 — situational

Electrolytes if you sweat buckets. Fibre if your diet is cardboard. Beta-alanine / citrulline if you like the niche benefit and already train hard. Multivitamin as chaotic-eater insurance — not a transformation plan.

### Tier 3 — skip for ROI

Fairy-dust fat burners. Most “test boosters.” Seventeen-ingredient pre-workouts that are just caffeine in a costume. Anything promising stage looks in a fortnight.

### What powders can’t do

Replace progressive overload. Fix weekend black holes. Spot-reduce a belly. Turn a bad programme into a good one.

## 2. WHY IT MATTERS

Beginners love stacks because stacks feel like progress. Smart trainees buy less and train harder. Get this hierarchy right before you graduate to bigger tools in the next chapters.

## 3. WHAT EXPERIENCED BODYBUILDERS DO

- Creatine + protein convenience + caffeine (if tolerated) covers most of the useful list.  
- They read labels for actual monohydrate — not mystery matrices.  
- They don’t expect pills to outrun sleep debt.  
- They laugh at the £200/month stack with no squat pattern.

## 4. YOUR ACTION PLAN

1. Lock food protein and your calorie bias for a few solid weeks.  
2. Add creatine monohydrate daily if you want the easy win (follow the label; this isn’t medical advice).  
3. Use powder only to fill gaps.  
4. Audit caffeine so afternoon hits aren’t wrecking sleep.  
5. Skip the fat-burner aisle.  
6. Get bloodwork before high-dose vitamin guesswork.
