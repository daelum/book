# THE 12-MONTH BODYBUILDER — Complete Outline

**Working title:** THE 12-MONTH BODYBUILDER  
**Promise:** Teach someone starting or restarting how to train, eat, recover, track, and decide what to ignore — so they can change their physique over twelve months with evidence-aware methods, not hype.

## How to use this book

- **Read Part One first** (everyone). It is the map of your body and your expectations.
- **Part Two** is the foundation toolkit: food, training, recovery, habits.
- **Part Three** is the 12-month roadmap — pick your pathway and execute.
- **Part Four** is literacy on supplements and advanced tools (educational / harm-reduction; beginner pathways stay drug-free).
- **Part Five** is identity, longevity, and what happens after month twelve.

### Reader pathways (A–D)

| ID | Pathway | Starting reality | Primary first-year bias |
|----|---------|------------------|-------------------------|
| **A** | Skinny / hardgainer look | Low body weight, low muscle, often low appetite | Surplus + progressive lifting; stop training like a dieter |
| **B** | Skinny-fat | Soft midsection + thin limbs / little muscle | Recomp or mild deficit + hard training; protein priority |
| **C** | Overweight / higher body fat | Significant fat to lose; may have some muscle underneath | Sustainable deficit + muscle retention; strength as non-negotiable |
| **D** | Already active / restart | Gym history, inconsistent results, or sport background | Fix the gaps (protein, progressive overload, tracking) before chasing novelty |

Secondary lenses (used inside chapters, not separate books): fat-loss focused · muscle-gain focused · informed curiosity about advanced tools.

### Standard chapter scaffold (where applicable)

## PART THREE — The 12-Month Roadmap

*Goal: Turn foundations into a year with phases, decision rules, and pathway-specific emphasis. Provisional weekly templates tighten as research matures.*

### Provisional month roadmap

| Phase | Months | Theme | Everyone does | Pathway tilt |
|-------|--------|-------|---------------|--------------|
| **Foundation** | 1–3 | Learn the craft | Form, protein/calorie literacy, sleep, basic progressive overload, photo + strength baselines | **A:** eat enough · **B:** protein + mild deficit/recomp · **C:** adherence-first deficit · **D:** audit & simplify |
| **Build** | 4–6 | Add productive stress | Volume progression, program structure locked, tracking quality, body-comp feedback loops | **A:** patient surplus · **B/C:** protect lifts while losing · **D:** progressive overload honesty |
| **Refine** | 7–9 | Aim the stimulus | Weak-point work, nutrition periodization (mini-cuts / growth blocks as needed), specialty techniques *as needed* | Pathway-specific weak points; avoid novelty addiction |
| **Peak / Decide** | 10–12 | Show the work + choose Year 2 | Photo/metric review, strength PRs vs Month 1, next-year plan, optional advanced-topic *literacy* (not prescriptions) | **A:** continue growth · **B/C:** decide recomp vs cut finish · **D:** periodize deliberately |
